How to play

Two pictures hang side by side — the left is the original, the right hides 7 sneaky shifts: things removed, recoloured, moved, resized, flipped or added. Click a difference on either picture to ring it in gold. Every difference is fair: big enough to see, small enough to miss. Six scenes, 42 differences in all — a dusk harbour, a night market, a coral garden, a sunny park picnic, a cozy library and a space station window. Wrong clicks cost 3 seconds; a hint (3 per scene) points at one hidden difference and costs 50 points.

Mouse: click to guess · hover = magnifier (when on) · Keys: ← ↑ ↓ → move the crosshair, Enter guess, Tab switch picture, H hint, L lens, N next scene, R restart, 1–6 jump to a scene.
